Nm7 Apply helper is installed

It types your own details from Nm7 into a company's job application form. You check everything and press Submit yourself. It never presses Submit or Next, never signs in and never touches a robot check.

  1. Connect. On nm7.si open Auto Apply 2.0, then Jobs ready, then Nm7 Apply helper, and tap Make a pairing code. Click the helper's icon in the toolbar (pin it from the puzzle-piece menu), paste the code under Pairing code from Nm7 and tap Connect.
  2. Open a job. In Jobs ready, tap Apply now. The company's apply page opens.
  3. Tap Fill. Click the helper's icon and tap Fill this page. Green fields are filled, yellow ones please check, red ones are left for you. On a form with several steps, press the page's own Next yourself, then tap Fill this step.

Want to try it first? Open a practice form of a made-up company. Nothing you type there is sent.

The helper talks only to Nm7. No analytics, no ads, no code from the internet. Privacy: nm7.si/privacy
